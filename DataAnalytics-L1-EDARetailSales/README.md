# EDA on Retail Sales Data

## Internship
- Intern: Dinesh Desale
- Track: Data Analytics
- Task: Level 1 – Task 1

## Objective
Perform exploratory data analysis on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer behaviour patterns, product performance, and actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset shape, data types and missing-value inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Monthly sales trend
- Quarterly sales trend
- Customer age-group analysis
- Gender breakdown
- Top 10 best-selling products
- Revenue by product category
- Correlation heatmap
- Additional demographic revenue visualization

## Key Results
- Total Revenue: ₹731,790
- Total Units Sold: 12,887
- Unique Customers: 183
- Average Transaction Value: ₹1,463.58

## Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize high-volume products and maintain adequate stock.
2. Focus inventory and promotional budgets on high-revenue categories.
3. Use customer demographic insights for targeted campaigns.
4. Plan inventory and promotions around seasonal sales trends.

## Conclusion
The analysis provides useful insights into sales performance, customer behaviour and product trends. These findings can support inventory planning, customer targeting and revenue-focused marketing decisions.
